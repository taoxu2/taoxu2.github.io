## Research Projects

### Spot–Futures Arbitrage in Apple Futures

This project studies whether weather and spot-price information can help detect mispricing and generate statistical arbitrage signals in the ZCE apple futures market.

### Optimization Approach to the LVDG Problem

This project studies the LVDG problem from an optimization perspective, focusing on how numerical optimization methods can be used to reformulate, solve, and analyze the original problem.
